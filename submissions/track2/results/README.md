# Track 2 — Results and submitted-stage evidence

The [preserved 14-study preliminary evidence extraction](G1_Focused_Scoping_Review_14_studies.csv) is available as a CSV with the recorded study-level fields, experimental models, conditions, outcomes and source references. It represents the **14 reports manually reviewed for the original Track 2 write-up**, not the later 108-publication synthesis.

The four final nominated pharmacological research hypotheses were **niacin, 4-phenylbutyrate, gentamicin and amlexanox**. **NMN was a mechanistic comparator**, not a fifth nominated candidate. See the [Track 2 overview](../README.md).

The subsequently developed [MVA scoping evidence review (108 publications)](../../../scoping%20review/README.md) and [Data extraction table](../../../scoping%20review/Data_Extraction_Table.csv) report the broader post-hackathon research. They must not be presented as the original submitted evidence.

**Provenance:** The preserved 14-study CSV is based on the saved research extraction file. A byte-identical match to a competition portal attachment has not been verified. The final submitted Track 2 report PDF is not currently available in this public directory. No patient-level genomic data are included.
